## Scientific data

All telescope images shown in the console are genuine cached NASA/IPAC IRSA
SPHEREx observations of the WISE 0855 field, bundle `real-wise-0855`.
They are not a new live acquisition or proof of detecting the named object.
DR1 source IDs: `2025W45_2A_0292_2`, `2025W50_2A_0479_1`,
`2026W18_2A_0663_1`. Actual dates: November 7 and December 13, 2025, and May 3,
2026. Full timestamps, approximate wavelengths, WCS and masks remain in the
repository's reviewed fixture manifests and exported example evidence.

- [SPHEREx mission](https://spherex.caltech.edu/)
- [IRSA SPHEREx DR1 documentation](https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/data/SPHEREx/docs/overview_dr1.html)
- [SPHEREx spectral images DOI](https://doi.org/10.26131/IRSA678)
- [Project scientific interpretation](../../docs/science-integrity.md)
- [Five-target evidence](../../docs/target-coverage.md)

This work uses the NASA/IPAC Infrared Science Archive, operated by the California
Institute of Technology under contract with NASA. NASA Space Apps participation
and use of NASA data do not imply NASA endorsement of Khonsu or its conclusions.

Quality overlays, estimated patches, optional refinement, known-source context
and original measurements are distinguished in the narration. The video does
not claim new optical resolution, calibrated variability or a Planet X discovery.
The recorded candidate note explicitly identifies itself as a tutorial example.

## Artwork, software and audio

- Khonsu wordmark, crescent, SVG guardian and star animation: original project
  artwork. Planet backdrop: existing AI-generated illustrative asset, documented
  in [the art-direction record](../../docs/art-direction.md). It is not science data.
- Barlow Condensed SemiBold: The Barlow Project Authors, SIL OFL 1.1. The font
  and its license are copied into `public/` from the existing project assets.
- Remotion 4.0.543: [documentation](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/cli/render),
  [license information](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/license/faq).
- React 19.2.0, Playwright and FFmpeg: video composition, local browser capture
  and media encoding. Their respective dependencies retain their license files.
- English narration: locally synthesized Microsoft Zira Desktop, using the
  installed Windows System.Speech engine. It is not a recording or imitation
  of any team member or performer.
- Quiet background ambience: original sinusoidal synthesis in `scripts/prepare.py`.
  No external song, stock soundtrack or sampled sound is used.
